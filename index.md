# Omni

Omni is a personal tool that its owner uses to publish his own videos on his own YouTube channels. It uploads each finished video to the owner's channel, schedules it, and reads the channel's view and retention statistics so the owner can improve future videos.

Omni is not a public service. It runs on the owner's own computer and is used only with the owner's own Google account and YouTube channels.

Omni es una herramienta personal: su dueño la usa para subir sus propios vídeos a sus propios canales de YouTube, programarlos y ver sus estadísticas.
