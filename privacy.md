# Omni - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Omni is a personal tool used by its owner to upload and schedule his own videos on his own YouTube channels and to read their statistics. This policy explains how Omni uses Google user data.

## Data Omni accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), Omni accesses: the basic information of the connected YouTube channel (name, ID and handle); the ability to upload videos, set their thumbnails and edit their titles and descriptions on that channel; and the channel's YouTube Analytics data (views and audience retention of its videos).

## How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos, to add links between his own videos, and to show the owner how his videos perform so he can make better ones. Omni does not use the data for advertising and does not use it to train general AI models.

## Storage

Everything is stored only on the owner's own computer: the access tokens in a local file and the statistics in a local database. Nothing is stored on any external server operated by Omni.

## Sharing

Omni does not sell, transfer or share Google user data with anyone. The data is only sent to Google's own APIs to perform the actions described above.

## Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can revoke Omni's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or disconnect each channel inside Omni. Deleting Omni's local files removes all stored data.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Omni's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
